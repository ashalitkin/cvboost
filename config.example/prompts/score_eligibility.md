ELIGIBLE = Jordan can realistically be engaged from Germany as a freelance contractor. Decide using:
- Location: "remote" that in fact requires US/UK/CA residence, a US state, citizenship or
  clearance -> not eligible. EU/EEA-wide or worldwide -> fine. USA: only fully remote AND
  B2B/contractor/EOR (no US work authorization); on-site or US-payroll employee only -> not
  eligible. A role based only in the home country is eligible only if the posting states a rate
  at or above the minimum. Hybrid, "x days in the office", on-site -> not eligible (an optional
  office with fully remote work is fine).
- Engagement: contract/freelance/B2B -> fine. "Full-time" in a remote worldwide/EU role of a
  foreign company is workload, not an employment contract: not a red flag by itself; say in
  summary_ru that the candidate should clarify or propose a B2B contract. "Employees only" is a
  red flag, not a rejection. NOT eligible: "no contractors", W2, US payroll, work authorization
  required, local entity employment only.
- Time zone: required overlap beyond what the candidate offers (a full US Eastern/Pacific day,
  APAC hours) -> not eligible; partial overlap -> fine, mention it.
- Rate: clearly below the minimum -> not eligible.
fit 0-100 = stack and seniority match for the best profile (senior roles fit; junior lower).
Roles centred on SQL, dbt and dashboards/BI are the top preference: higher fit. Platform/infra
heavy roles (Spark, Kafka, Kubernetes, heavy software engineering) get a lower fit.
FIT IS ONLY STACK + SENIORITY + FOCUS. Do NOT lower fit for a single missing tool, "full-time",
no rate stated or time-zone notes: those go to red_flags/summary_ru.
profile = best matching profile, or "none".
