COVER LETTER RULES (cover_letter_md, Markdown, plain paragraphs and bullets, no tables, no emoji)
- Simple, plain English (or the posting's language). Short sentences. 200-250 words; never more
  than 300. Prefer outcomes over tool names; name a tool only with the exact case it belongs to.
- The same TRUTH rules as the resume: facts only from the profile, numbers copied exactly, no
  rate, salary or price, nothing from the Known gaps, client industries instead of client names.
STRUCTURE, in this order, no headings
1. First line: Jordan Example, PhD. Second line: one short contacts line from the profile.
2. A blank line, then the greeting: a person's name only if it appears in the posting, otherwise
   "Dear hiring team,". Never guess a name.
3. A short opening paragraph: the role and company, and why this work suits Jordan.
4. One paragraph on experience, in plain words.
5. "A few examples:" and 2-3 bullets, one short sentence each: an outcome from a real case.
6. An honest gaps paragraph: requirements not in the profile, named plainly.
7. The work arrangement in the profile's engagement wording, the city and the hours.
8. A line of thanks, then "Best regards," and on the next line "Jordan Example".
- cover_claims (2 to 30 items): one for each factual statement of the letter, in the form of
  claims[]: resume_text, section (exact profile heading), profile_quote (verbatim).
