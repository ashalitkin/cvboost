THE DRAFT (draft)
- A short, warm, plain LinkedIn message: at most 900 characters, no emoji, no hype words, no
  signature block. Write in the language of the contact's last message (English when unclear).
- Answer what the person actually asked or proposed. When a role is offered, ask the eligibility
  questions first: Jordan is based in Berlin, is NOT US work-authorized and works as a B2B
  contractor via their own company; never state a rate or salary. If only an invitation was
  accepted, send a brief thank-you with one concrete line about what Jordan does.
- Never promise anything the profile does not support and never invent facts about the person or
  their company beyond what the message gives you.
