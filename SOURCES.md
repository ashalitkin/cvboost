# Sources

Generated by `cvboost sources --markdown` from the connector code and `docs/sources_meta.yaml`. Do not edit by hand.

Every source is a small connector. They prefer official APIs and feeds, wait politely between requests and identify themselves with a generic User-Agent. LinkedIn, Indeed and Glassdoor pages are never scraped: use their job-alert e-mails (`email_alerts`).

Most sources need nothing from you. Three need a free key, one needs a mailbox, and the company career pages need a list of companies in `config/companies.yaml`.

## remotive

A small curated list of remote jobs from Remotive (a handful of postings, delayed by 24 hours).

- **Kind:** JSON API, `https://remotive.com/api/remote-jobs`
- **Key or setup needed:** none
- **Price:** Free, no account.
- **Limits:** The site asks for at most 4 requests a day; CVBoost asks every 6 hours.
- **Runs:** every 6 h, on by default
- **Terms of service:** Link to the Remotive URL and name Remotive as the source; no re-posting on third-party sites; data delayed 24h. robots.txt disallows /api/*, but the documented API is the one used here.
- **Attribution in the panel:** via Remotive
- **Note:** Postings are shown with a link back to Remotive. Applying may require an account on the site.

## email_alerts

Job-alert e-mails from LinkedIn, Indeed, Dice, Freelancermap and other senders, read from a mailbox you dedicate to this.

- **Kind:** job-alert e-mails, `imaps://imap.gmail.com`
- **Key or setup needed:** `CVBOOST_IMAP_USER`, `CVBOOST_IMAP_PASSWORD`
- **Where to get it:** Create saved searches with e-mail alerts on those sites, forward them to a dedicated Gmail account, create an App password for it (Google account, Security, 2-step verification, App passwords) and put CVBOOST_IMAP_USER and CVBOOST_IMAP_PASSWORD into .env.
- **Price:** Free (a Gmail account).
- **Limits:** The mailbox is read once per cycle, read-only. Unknown senders go through a capped LLM fallback (30 calls a day by default).
- **Runs:** every 10 min, on by default
- **Terms of service:** Own mailbox, read-only IMAP. Platform alert mails are parsed deterministically; links are never opened. Unknown senders go to a capped LLM fallback.
- **Attribution in the panel:** via e-mail alerts
- **Note:** This is how LinkedIn and Indeed are covered without scraping them. Links in the mails are never opened. Use a dedicated mailbox, never your main one.
